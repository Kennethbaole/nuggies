What goes in a notes file?

A plain .txt file, written however you like. Pronouns, nicknames, age group, tone, topics you share, things to avoid. You can also paste a few messages you’ve sent them; replies borrow the style, not the words.

Maya is my younger sister. She uses she/her pronouns. I call her May. We’re close, and I usually speak casually with her. Keep my replies short, warm, and sometimes playful. Light teasing is fine, but avoid overly sentimental wording. We often talk about food and movies.

Notes are guidance, not a record of how you feel. They are saved on the computer running Glint and sent only to the reply model it is set up to use. Nothing is trained on them.
